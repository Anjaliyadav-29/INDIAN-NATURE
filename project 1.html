🌎INDIAN NATURE☘️🍃

india is a country blessed with beautiful and diverse nature. It has high mountains, green forests, rivers, lakes, deserts, waterfalls, and long coastlines. The Himalayas in the north are home to many plants and animals, while the forests provide shelter to wildlife such as tigers elephants, deer, and many species of birds. Rivers like the Ganga Yamuna, and Narmada are important sources of water and support agriculture and human life. India’s natural beauty attracts people from all over the world. Nature also gives us fresh air, clean water, food, and many useful resources. Therefore, it is our responsibility to protect forests, wildlife, rivers, and other natural resources for future generations...☘️
